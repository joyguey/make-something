# Rainforest set dressing — provenance

- **Made by:** procedural Blender script `ArtSource/Rainforest/build_rainforest.py` (Blender 4.2.1 LTS, headless). Editable source: `ArtSource/Rainforest/Rainforest.blend`.
- **Style:** original shapes in a bright, cute, thick-outlined 2D-game look requested by the owner ("similar to MapleStory"). Nothing is traced, copied or downloaded from MapleStory or any other game; no external models, textures or fonts.
- **Licence:** project-owned, same terms as the rest of this repository.
- Rebuild: `Blender --background --factory-startup --python ArtSource/Rainforest/build_rainforest.py`, then `RoomOrchestra.EditorTools.LittleEchoesSceneSetup.ApplyAndSave()` in Unity.
