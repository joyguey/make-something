# Little Echoes v6.1: three ways in, and a smoother Story

**Title sign: Story, Sandbox or Play together.** The opening sign now offers three choices, each with a handprint to high-five (or press with a controller). Quit is the small plate in the corner.
- **Story** goes straight into Story mode; the Sandbox's opening and lesson wait until you choose Sandbox.
- **Leaving Story** brings the sign back, so you can choose Sandbox or Play together.
- In the Sandbox, **Story** is on the menu's first row (the floating Play Story sign is gone). Collected instrument cards are on Story's first card.

**Story mode**
- **The Grumble fight:** when the friend on your palm can't crack the current shell, the Grumble sends out a little Discord that friend can beat, so every flick has a target.
- **Easy aim** makes a clear difference: on, flicks find their target and Discords move slower; off, you aim yourself.
- **Warnings** before the room fills with Discord, and a plainer message if it does.
- **Buttons** under the portal can be pinched during play: look at one for a moment, then pinch.
- **Flicks** that peaked before the finger left the thumb now count.
- Story's music always uses the Handpan.
- **Chinese** text for all of Story mode (a native speaker review is still to come).

**Play together (Echo Battle), from reviewer feedback**
- Throws pass through the circling echo friends (they no longer block a throw).
- The caller gets a tune idea each round (Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Hot Cross Buns, Row Your Boat, Frère Jacques, Old MacDonald).
- The room scan no longer sits at 0%: it says why and moves on.

**Fixed:** leaving Story could place an instrument in the Sandbox behind it.

Version 6.1 (code 45).
