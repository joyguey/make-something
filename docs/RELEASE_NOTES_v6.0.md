# Little Echoes v6.0: Story mode

**Story mode (new).** A portal opens in your room onto a meadow the Grumble has drained of colour and song. Flick Flutter, Bop and Boom from your open palm at the Discords that come through it: Flutter pierces Squeaks, Bop bounces to Wobblers, Boom's shockwave cracks Clangs. Every Discord you beat becomes a note that brings the meadow's colour back, while Flutter, Bop and Boom journey to the World Tree.

- **Three levels, three instruments,** each with a card of its real history:
  1. **The Seed**: the Udu, a clay pot drum of the Igbo people of southeastern Nigeria.
  2. **The Two Strings**: the Erhu, a two-stringed fiddle from China (at dusk).
  3. **The World Tree**: the Handpan, a tuned steel instrument from Switzerland (at night).
- **A real flick:** curl your finger against your thumb beside the friend, then snap it out. Flip your left hand over and back to swap friends. Controllers: point and pull the trigger.
- **Harmony** charges on a watch around your right wrist; streaks fill it faster; full, your next flick is a Chorus.
- **Easy aim**, Pause and Leave are always on the buttons under the portal.
- Restoring an instrument is a bonus you can view from the Sandbox (the gold card by the Play Story sign); nothing in the Sandbox is ever locked.

**Sandbox.** The Handpan now plays on the chair (the Mbira is still there with Change). Flutter is gentler on the Handpan.
